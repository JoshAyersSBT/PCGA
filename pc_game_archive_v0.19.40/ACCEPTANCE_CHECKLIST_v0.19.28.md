# v0.19.28 video player acceptance

- [ ] Open **Movies & TV**, play a Jellyfin movie, and enter fullscreen using its control (or `F`).
- [ ] The fullscreen progress slider takes most of the available horizontal space; time labels occupy only their content widths.
- [ ] Leave the pointer and keyboard still for approximately three seconds while playing: controls, stream badge and cursor disappear.
- [ ] Move pointer or tap the player: controls reappear; keyboard `F`, `Space`/`K` and `M` remain functional.
- [ ] Pause the movie: controls stay visible. Resume: they hide again after inactivity.
- [ ] Scrub or focus the seek range by keyboard: controls remain usable, then hide after activity ends.
- [ ] Exit fullscreen with Esc: controls stay visible in normal mode.
- [ ] Repeat on a tablet/mobile viewport and a 1080p+ screen.
- [ ] Confirm progress reporting, subtitle selection, audio selection, and playback position are unchanged.
